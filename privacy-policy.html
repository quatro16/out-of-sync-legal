Privacy Policy

Application: OUT OF SYNC · Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quatro Fan ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile game OUT OF SYNC (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by Quatro Fan. For any questions about this Policy or about your data, contact us at: quatro.fan.game@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email, or other personal contact details to play. The App collects the following categories of data automatically or through third-party services:

  • Advertising data. We show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information (model, operating system, language), and your interaction with ads.
  • Diagnostics data. The App is built with the Unity engine, which may send technical crash and performance reports (for example device model, operating system version, and error details) to Unity so that we can find and fix problems. These reports are not used to identify you personally.
  • Local data on your device. Your best streak, Daily Challenge results, tutorial progress, and settings (music, sound effects, language) are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location (GPS), or payment information. The App does not access your camera or microphone. The current version of the App contains no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

3. How we use the data

  • To operate the game and remember your progress and settings;
  • To show advertising, which keeps the App free;
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the stability of the App;
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

4. Legal basis and your consent (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using a consent form provided through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can choose your preferences and change them later at any time from the App (Settings → Privacy Settings; this option is shown in regions where such a choice is required). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. We also process certain data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating, securing, and improving the App.

5. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Technologies (engine and diagnostics) — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

6. Advertising choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising at the device level. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization". Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can also change their consent choice at any time in the App.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet and advertising ID. Used by the advertising service and for diagnostics described above. The App does not request any other device permissions.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not create user accounts and do not store your personal data on our own servers. Diagnostics data is retained by Unity, and advertising data by Google, according to their own policies. Data stored locally on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. You can also reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at quatro.fan.game@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Data security

We rely on the security measures of Google and Unity, including encryption of data in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own privacy frameworks and safeguards.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
